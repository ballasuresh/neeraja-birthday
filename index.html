My dearest Neeraja,

Happy birthday. I made this so you would have a place that holds your name the way I hold it — carefully, proudly, and a little in awe. If you are reading this today, tomorrow, or years from now, it is still for you.

You are the quiet I look for in a loud room. The smile I replay when the day is heavy. You turned ordinary hours into a life I want to keep.

September 3 will always be gold on my calendar. The world can wait. This hour — this one, right now — is only for you.

Happy birthday, my love. May every year be as gentle as you are.

Always yours,
Sooraj
